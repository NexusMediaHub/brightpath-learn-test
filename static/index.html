How an answer gets checked

A small language model writes the answer. Plain code then checks it before you see it, so a confident guess never reaches the page.

  1. ReadEvery question is sent with all three documents. They are short, so nothing is searched or left out.
  2. CheckSources that don't exist are dropped. An answer that shares no words with its source is thrown away.
  3. AnswerYou get the answer and its document, or a plain "not in the documents" and the support address.

Support is open Monday to Friday, 09:00–17:00 UTC.

Pricing, refunds and job placement are not in the help documents. Ask support.
